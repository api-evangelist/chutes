Artificial intelligence (AI) requires processing enormous amounts of data and performing millions of calculations at once. Traditional central processing units (CPUs) are not optimized for this level of parallel processing. Instead, AI uses graphics processing units (GPUs), which are specifically designed to handle thousands of simultaneous computations — making them essential for modern AI applications.
